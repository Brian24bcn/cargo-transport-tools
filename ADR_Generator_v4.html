✅ ADR Preparation Checklist
To be checked from the documentation:
1) Transport document or transport according to ADR 1.1.4.2.1 with DGD
2) Dangerous goods are already on the transport unit and calculated
3) Each crew member has a valid driving licence and photo ID
4) Shipments of Class 1: valid certificate of competence available
5) For Class 7 shipments: transport authorisation available
6) Total >1000 points or Class 7: Valid training certificate (ADR 8.2)
7) Written instructions
To be checked by the loader:
8) Only undamaged packages handed over & segregation observed
9) Transport ≤ 1000 points
a) 1 x fire extinguisher min. 2 kg, sealed and inspected
10) Transport > 1000 points or Class 7:
a) Marking of the transport unit front and rear with orange plates
b) Class 1 and Class 7: additionally placards on the sides and rear
c) Fire extinguishers (capacity per tonnage), sealed and inspected
d) Protective equipment according to written instructions
e) Prohibition of passenger transport observed
11) No obvious defects found in load securing or on the vehicle
12) Visual inspection carried out for dangerous goods already loaded
