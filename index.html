📋 ADR Transport Tool ADR 2025 · Road · EN / DE

Calculates ADR exemption points (1.1.3.6) across multiple UN substances and generates the Transport Document and Driver Checklist as PDFs. Includes an editable UN database with class, packing group, tunnel code and transport category.

Requires: template_adr_form.pdf · template_adr_checklist.pdf

🚛 CMR Smart Issuer CMR Convention · Road · EN

Fills and exports CMR International Consignment Notes with digital signature. Generates 4 official copies (sender, consignee, carrier, driver) in one PDF. Address book with quick-select autocomplete per destination.

Requires: template_cmr.pdf

✈️ DGR Acceptance Hub IATA DGR · Air Cargo · EN / DE

Fills IATA DGR acceptance checklists and generates rejection notices with photo evidence. Supports With DGD and Without DGD (Non-Radioactive) forms. Multi-airline profiles and shipper database included.

Requires: Your airline's checklist PDF · template_rejection_document.pdf

⚙️ How to use

1

Open the tool you need by clicking the button above. It runs directly in your browser — no installation needed.

2

Upload the PDF template when the tool asks for it. The templates are in the templates/ folder of this repository.

3

Fill in the shipment details using the form. Your address book and settings are saved locally in your browser.

4

Generate the PDF. The filled document downloads automatically. No data is sent anywhere.

5

Back up your data regularly using the 📥 Export button in each tool. This saves your address books as a JSON file you can restore on any device.
